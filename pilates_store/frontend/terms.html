01About these terms

These Pre-booking Terms & Conditions (“terms”) apply to every order placed on this website. “We”, “us” and “our” mean [Registered business name], [registered address], GSTIN [GSTIN]. “You” means the person placing the order.

When you tick “I have read and agree to the Pre-booking Terms & Conditions” at checkout and confirm your pre-booking, you agree to these terms. Please read them before you pay. If anything is unclear, contact us before you order.

02What pre-booking means

We do not keep finished stock. Each piece is built, finished and quality-checked after it is ordered. A pre-booking reserves your piece in our next production batch at today’s price.

  • Your pre-booking is confirmed once we receive the pre-booking amount (or the full amount, if you choose to pay in full).
  • You will receive a booking ID (for example PB-123456) by email. Keep it — you will need it to make changes.
  • A pre-booking is a binding order for the products, setup option and quantity shown at checkout, subject to your right to cancel before dispatch (section 8).

03Batches and sold-out batches

We build in monthly batches. Each batch has a limited number of places. On every product page you will see the batches:

  • Sold out — the batch is fully booked and cannot be chosen.
  • Pre-book — the open batch. Your order joins this batch. Right now this is the next batch, with an estimated delivery of 3–4 weeks from today.

The batch and estimated delivery dates shown at checkout are the ones that apply to your order.

04The pre-booking amount (part payment)

At checkout you choose one of two payment options:

  • Part payment — pay 10% of your order total today (including any shipping charge, rounded up to the nearest rupee). The rest is paid before dispatch.
  • Pay in full — pay the whole amount today. Nothing is due later.

The pre-booking amount is not a fee or a deposit you lose. It is part of the price and is adjusted against your final bill. Your invoice will show the full price, the amount already paid, and the balance.

05Paying the balance

  1. Day 0You pre-book and pay 10%. Your price is locked.
  2. Weeks 1–3Your batch is built and quality-checked. We send updates.
  3. Before dispatchWe email and text you a secure link to pay the balance.
  4. Weeks 3–4Your order is dispatched and delivered.
  • Please pay the balance within 7 days of receiving the payment link. We will send reminders.
  • Your order is dispatched only after the balance is paid in full.
  • If the balance is not paid within 7 days, we may cancel the pre-booking. If we do, we refund the pre-booking amount in full — nothing is kept.

06Price protection

The price shown at checkout is locked when you pre-book. It will not increase before delivery, even if our prices go up for later batches. Prices include applicable taxes. The pre-order discount shown against the MRP applies only to the batch and order it was offered for.

07Delivery timeline and delays

  • Orders are delivered within an estimated 3–4 weeks of the date you pre-book. The exact window is shown on the product page, in your cart and at checkout.
  • This is an estimate, not a guaranteed date. We will tell you as soon as we know of any change.
  • If delivery is going to be more than 14 days later than the end of your estimated window, you can choose to wait or to cancel for a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • We deliver across India. Free shipping applies to orders of ₹999 or more; smaller orders have a ₹99 shipping charge, shown at checkout. International delivery is quoted separately.
  • Delays caused by events outside our reasonable control (for example natural disasters, strikes or courier disruption) extend the estimated window, and we will keep you informed.

08Changing or cancelling a pre-booking

Before dispatch you can:

  • change your delivery address or phone number;
  • change the setup option or quantity, if places are still available in your batch (any price difference is added to or taken off your balance);
  • cancel your pre-booking for a full refund of everything you have paid. No reason needed.

To make a change, contact us with your booking ID (section 16). After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled; the returns policy in section 12 applies instead.

09Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of the cancellation being confirmed. Your bank may take a few more days to show the money in your account. We never deduct a cancellation fee from a pre-booking cancelled before dispatch.

10If we have to cancel

In rare cases we may need to cancel a pre-booking — for example if we cannot make the product to our quality standard, if your address is outside our delivery area, or if a payment cannot be verified. If this happens we will tell you why and refund everything you have paid in full.

11Receiving your order

  • Someone aged 18 or over should be available to receive the delivery. We will agree a delivery day with you.
  • Please check the packaging when it arrives. Report any damage or missing parts within 48 hours, with photos, and we will repair or replace the affected parts free of charge.
  • Large equipment arrives 90% assembled, with tools and a step-by-step guide.

12Returns after delivery

  • Accessories (mats, balls, bands, ropes, bottles and similar) can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in their original packaging.
  • Because they are built to order, reformers and other large equipment can be returned only if they arrive damaged or faulty.
  • Approved refunds follow the timeline in section 9.

13Warranty

Frames are covered for 5 years. Springs, ropes and upholstery are covered for 2 years against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or damage from not following the care guide. Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.

14Payments and your data

We accept cards, UPI and net banking through a secure payment provider. We do not store your card details. We use your contact and address details only to process and deliver your order and to send you updates about it.

15Changes to these terms and governing law

We may update these terms from time to time. The version shown when you placed your pre-booking applies to that order. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts of [city] have jurisdiction.
